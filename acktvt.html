Security standards

Built in line with recognised standards

acktvt is designed to the requirements of the standards below. We do not hold a certification against them today: where a standard is listed, it means our controls were built to it, not that an auditor has certified acktvt.

DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

India's data protection law. The reasonable security safeguards of Rule 6: encryption, access control, logs kept for at least a year, and backups, so that data can be protected and what happened can be shown later.

OWASP Top 10

The widely used list of web application risks. Protection against injection and cross-site scripting, a strict content security policy, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and security checks before each release.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A

The international information security standard. Our controls are mapped to its themes of access control, cryptography, logging, backup and supplier relationships.

Industry-standard cryptography

HTTPS for every connection; AES-256-GCM for sensitive personal data fields and for backups; passwords stored only as bcrypt hashes; SHA-256 fingerprints for tamper evidence.

What that means in practice

  • Your data kept apart: each Business Management Suite client runs on its own database and server; every Data Protection Suite workspace is separated by row-level security
  • Protected sign-in: limits on repeated attempts everywhere; in the Data Protection Suite, two-step verification with an authenticator app for administrators and one active session per user; in the Business Management Suite, a captcha at sign-in and password renewal every 180 days
  • Personal data encrypted field by field in the Business Management Suite, so it stays unreadable even inside a backup
  • Sealed backups that detect any change to the file before a restore
  • A record of every change: full history on every record, and an append-only audit log kept at least 12 months in the Data Protection Suite
  • Tamper evidence you can prove: acktvt Genuine and Origin stamp each day's fingerprints on the Bitcoin blockchain; only fingerprints leave acktvt, and anyone can verify them with free tools
  • Leaving is simple: when a Data Protection Suite subscription ends, a 30-day window to export your records, then deletion
