The product family

Business Management Suite & Data Protection Suite

Toggle on only what your business needs today, and add modules as you grow.

acktvt Business Management Suite

A modular ERP for growing Indian businesses: warehouse and stock, production, project reporting, resource management, e-invoicing & e-way bill, Tally export, alerts & scheduling, predictive intelligence and secure backup & data export — mandatory core modules plus optional add-ons, priced by seat-count tier so you only pay for what you switch on.

Learn more on acktvt.com →

acktvt Data Protection Suite

Compliance-readiness software for India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act — built first for hospitals, nursing homes and diagnostic labs, and expanding sector by sector. Covers your RoPA/data-map register, consent notices, vendor & DPA tracking, breach-response workflow and grievance SLA tracking, with statutory essentials available from the base tier.
