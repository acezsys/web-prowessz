Founder & Managing Partner

Siddhesh S. Zare

Siddhesh conceived Prowessz Consulting from a career spanning technology and project & programme management across the globe, working with eminent organisations along the way.

"Being the catalyst in a client's business" is the standard he holds the firm to — and the reason clients extend their trust to Prowessz Consulting.

"Looking at what businesses actually need today, an extraordinary throughput alone isn't a game-changer any more — the results have to line up with what the client expected." He insists on understanding the business context in real detail before any solutioning begins, because client satisfaction starts the moment they first connect with the firm.

Co-founder & Partner

Meghanaa

From an electronics-engineering background, Meghanaa began in motherboard production and testing at a global healthcare-technology company, later moving into quality control — testing heavy healthcare machines post-production and driving internal quality checks.

She helped create and update product manuals and documentation to meet regulatory standards across the EU and the Americas. Alongside the quality dimension of client projects, she has a strong people orientation, making her a communicative, people-first leader within the firm.

Team Prowessz sees her as the leader who builds the bond of trust with stakeholders — and an impactful working environment in the years ahead.
