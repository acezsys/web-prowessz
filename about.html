Who we are

Rooted in one operator's experience, built for many industries

Prowessz Consulting is a Management & Technology consulting firm recognised by DPIIT, Government of India. It grew out of the hands-on experience of an IT and project-management professional, and today serves clients across information technology, project and programme management, strategy, marketing and human resources — with stakeholder management woven through every engagement.

Under the leadership of Siddhesh S. Zare, the firm works across demographics and functions rather than a single industry vertical, applying the same disciplined approach wherever the client's growth is stalling.

Our mission

Turn every over-run into a growth engine

We aim to set a benchmark for project excellence — converting a stalled function or an over-running project into momentum, rather than treating it as a one-off rescue. Our methods and practices are adapted to each client's growth trajectory, not applied as a fixed template.
