Where MSMEs typically get stuck

Seven challenges we see most often

  1. 01

    Access to top-tier expertise

    Big-4 consulting firms are often out of reach on brand recognition, cost, scope or geography — even when the need for real stakeholder management is just as pressing.

  2. 02

    No process, or the wrong process

    Sometimes having no process is better than having a bad one. Spotting and correcting a process that's actively hurting productivity is a job most MSMEs can't do from the inside.

  3. 03

    Operations-only focus

    Real business compulsions keep transformation a distant priority for years — often at the cost of customer satisfaction (CSAT), employee development, technology, and cyber security.

  4. 04

    Centralised decision-making

    One person often has to run the show, which creates a bottleneck where only the loudest priorities get attention.

  5. 05

    Post-sales gaps

    Weak after-sales service damages CSAT and reputation — and that damage lingers, affecting revenue and market entry long after the original issue is fixed.

  6. 06

    Cost and demand pressure

    Process discipline, employee wellbeing and talent retention matter most exactly when demand is soft and competition is tightest.

  7. 07

    Limited funding avenues

    Investors want long-term growth, global positioning and stronger brand value — which itself calls for the transformation and stakeholder management many MSMEs can't yet fund.
